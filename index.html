Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Email us at pracima.help@gmail.com

What types of charities can I donate to, and how do I find them?

There are many types of charities out there, ranging from local organizations to large international ones. You can donate to charities that focus on a particular cause, such as animal welfare.

Is my donation tax-deductible, and how do I claim it on my taxes?

It depends on the charity's registration and the tax rules in your country. Every donation on Pracima comes with a receipt you can keep for your records and share with your tax advisor.

Can I donate anonymously?

Yes. When you donate you can choose to hide your name, so only the amount is shown publicly on the fundraiser.

What percentage of my donation actually goes to the charity after administrative costs?

Each fundraiser page shows how funds are used. Most charities publish yearly reports that break down programme and administrative spending.

Can I donate goods or services instead of money?

Many charities accept goods, food or volunteer time. Check the fundraiser page or contact the charity directly to see what they need most.
